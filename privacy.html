Study Coach - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026. Study Coach (https://celtics24.github.io) is a study-summaries and exam-tracking web app for students. Contact: y0546367987@gmail.com

What we store

Your profile is stored on our server (Cloudflare) and on devices you connect with your sync code: first and last name, subjects, exams, links to your summaries, timetable, notebooks and text pages you create, theme, study streak and marks. If you turn on notifications, a technical push address is stored. Linked files stay in your own Drive with your own permissions.

Abuse prevention and short-lived records

To limit abuse we keep counters in short windows (15 minutes) tied to a hashed network address or profile, and a daily count of photo scans per profile that resets each day (UTC). These counters contain no images, text or calendar data, and expired entries are removed automatically or cleaned up periodically. Reminder delivery logs are kept for 48 hours. Friend-sharing approval records are kept 30 to 40 days.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with other users unless you opt in. If you make yourself searchable, friends can find your name; if you also turn on name-prefix search and suggestions, only your first name and the first letter of your last name are shown, plus a count of mutual friends. Accepted friends see only the names of your notebooks and summaries. Content is copied only with your approval of each item, and the copy stays with the person who received it. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

Photo to text

If you choose to scan a board photo, the image is sent for one-time processing by an AI model on Cloudflare Workers AI to produce text. The app does not store the image or write it to its own logs.

Google Calendar import (optional)

If you tap "Import exams from Google Calendar", the app asks for one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.owned.readonly (read events on calendars you own). The app reads events on your primary Google Calendar only (not other calendars) for the next 14 months, in your browser, and shows only those whose titles look like exams so you can pick which to import.

  • The access token stays in your browser's memory only, and is revoked right after the read. No refresh token is requested or stored.
  • Event data is processed in your browser. Nothing from your calendar is sent to our server or saved unless you tick an event and tap import. Only the ticked exams (subject, title, date) are saved to your private profile, like an exam you added by hand. They are not shown to friends.
  • We do not write to, change or delete anything in your calendar.
  • Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not shown to other users, not read by people, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models, and not sent to any AI model. The exams you choose to import are stored with our hosting provider, Cloudflare, which acts as our service provider to run the app, like any other profile data. We disclose them to no one else, except where needed for security or to comply with the law.

Study Coach's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete profile removes your profile, content, notification subscriptions and friend links from our server, and the copy on that device. It does not delete files in your Drive, copies already given to friends, or copies on your other devices. You can revoke Study Coach's Google access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask for deletion by email, write to y0546367987@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this page and its date if these practices change.

Optional study chat

Conversation with ai coach is optional. Only a saved text summary you select, your question, and up to three previous exchanges (limited to 6,000 characters) are processed temporarily on Cloudflare Workers AI, after your consent. Drive links are not fetched, and calendar data and other profiles are excluded. The chat is kept only in device memory and clears when you leave its page or start a new chat. The app does not store chats or log their content. Cloudflare does not use standard inference content for training and does not retain it after processing, subject to security and legal exceptions. Daily counters without chat content are kept until the daily reset. AI answers can be wrong; verify against your study material. Do not enter personal or sensitive information. The free pilot is capped at three requests per profile per UTC day and a small shared global quota. Requests may stop when a quota is exhausted; no paid plan is enabled for this feature.

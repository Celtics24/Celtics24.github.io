Study Coach

Study Coach is a free web app for students. Keep summaries, notebooks per subject and exam dates in one place, get reminders before exams, and share notebook names with friends you approve.

Optional: import exam dates from your own Google Calendar. The app asks for read-only access to events on your primary calendar, shows the events that look like exams, and saves only the ones you tick. Access is revoked right after reading.
